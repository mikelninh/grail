# Golden Cases

## GC-1 — Semantic mint beats mundane floor
Spider-Man #1962 at a small premium may outrank an ordinary mint at floor because 1962 is the character's first-appearance year; price penalty and semantic reason remain separate.

## GC-2 — Mint hype cannot justify nonsense price
A semantic #1962 listing at 3x recent reference must not rank as a strong opportunity merely because its mint is meaningful.

## GC-3 — Floor value is not liquidation value
Ten assets showing a $500 floor but median sales near $200 and one sale/month must produce liquidation value far below $5,000.

## GC-4 — Graph evidence is traversable
`mint:1962 -> matches_year -> year:1962 -> first_appearance_of -> Spider-Man` must be retrievable.

## Future live golden case
Blind-test 100+ live/archived listings against (A) lowest ask, (B) deepest floor discount and (C) GRAIL rank, with collector labels hidden until ranking is frozen.
