# Constraints

- Read-only intelligence first; no autonomous purchases.
- Never promise profit or label a candidate a guaranteed snipe.
- Separate opportunity score from confidence.
- Preserve source, timestamp and evidence for production observations.
- Respect platform terms, rate limits and authentication boundaries.
- Normalise OMI/Gem/USD and fees at observation time.
- Classify custody/escrow/platform wallets before ownership analysis.
- Floor can be manipulated or thin; liquidation value requires depth/sales evidence.
- Core scoring runs without an LLM.
- Every scoring rule needs a unit test or golden case.
- Adapters remain replaceable; no secrets in repository.
