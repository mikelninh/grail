# Hunter selection logic

Hunter ranks only current live candidates from GRAIL / EDGE / WATCH. Ranking considers opportunity score, GRAIL status, mint score, discount magnitude and evidence confidence. Ownership-source conflicts reduce rank. The same collectible is deduplicated and no universe may occupy all three slots when alternatives exist.

Generic patterns are never sufficient to create a GRAIL thesis on their own. Hunter may show an EDGE with an ordinary mint, but the card must say explicitly that the thesis is price rather than mint collectibility.
