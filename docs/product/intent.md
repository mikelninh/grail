# Intent

## Product thesis
GRAIL helps serious digital collectors discover mispriced or semantically underappreciated VeVe/StackR assets before simple floor-price tools do.

## User
A collector with $1k-$100k+ exposure who values mint numbers, provenance, scarcity, market structure and IP significance—not just floor charts.

## Job to be done
Given live listings and historical/on-chain evidence, rank opportunities by expected collector-interest asymmetry and explain why each candidate is unusual.

## Non-goals for v0.1
- Autonomous buying or custody.
- Price guarantees or investment advice.
- Opaque LLM-generated valuations.
- Full VeVeVerse integration.

## Success
On a held-out sample of real listings, GRAIL should surface meaningful mints / discrepancies in the top results more often than baseline sorting by cheapest ask or floor discount alone.
